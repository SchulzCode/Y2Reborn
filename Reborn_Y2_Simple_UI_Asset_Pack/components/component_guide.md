# Reborn Y2 — Simple UI component guide

## Intent
Fewer simultaneous controls, more whitespace, stronger single-focus navigation, and lower rendering cost than the first Reborn concept.

## Status bar
- 28 px
- non-focusable
- Reborn at left; output/battery at right

## Focus
- 2 px #FFE2A4 outline
- subtle #201C16 fill
- optional 4 px glow
- exactly one focus target

## Rows
- 42–48 px high
- 15 px primary text
- 12 px secondary text
- right-aligned value/duration

## Footer
- 36 px
- volume left
- format center
- output right

## Now Playing
- album art ~164×164
- title 24–26 px
- artist 18 px
- progress 4 px
- playback controls reflect hardware state rather than touch targets

## Library
Maximum 2 cover columns. For fastest wheel navigation, a list is preferable.
